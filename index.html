Master's Thesis
3D MRI Radiogenomics for MGMT Status Prediction in Glioblastoma
IIIT Bhubaneswar · Master's Thesis · Grade: 10/10
2022 – 2023
  • Developed an end-to-end pipeline to non-invasively predict MGMT promoter methylation — a key predictive biomarker in Glioblastoma — using the UPenn-GBM dataset (630 subjects, 155 MRI slices each).
  • Extracted multimodal imaging features from multi-parametric MRI (T1, T2, T1-CE, T2-FLAIR) via transfer learning with pretrained CNN architectures (AlexNet, VGGNet).
  • Applied Recursive Feature Elimination (RFE) to reduce redundancy in high-dimensional imaging representations and improve model interpretability.
  • Designed SVM classifiers with Stratified K-Fold Cross-Validation and Grid Search, optimizing ROC-AUC on heterogeneous imaging data.
  • Research supports precision oncology by enabling non-invasive biomarker discovery as an alternative to surgical biopsy.
